About

Growth with margin discipline

25+ years in projects and key account management, 20+ of them in power generation and 16 in international markets. I build international businesses in power generation: entering new markets, developing distributor networks, and growing revenue without giving up margin.

At Kirloskar Oil Engines I own the international P&L across 50+ markets in Africa, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, Europe and the Americas, and partner with the CEO and leadership team on market entry, portfolio direction and competitive positioning.

Before that, I spent 17 years at Mahindra Powerol, taking greenfield markets from entry to scale and winning multi-country telecom accounts. My work centres on competing against low-cost OEMs through smarter pricing, specification and portfolio strategy, and on leading cross-functional teams across cultures.
